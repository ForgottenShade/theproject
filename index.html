Ananas aus Caracas

A tiny roguelike built with rot.js

Arrows Numpad WASD move. Home PgUp End PgDn (or numpad 7 9 1 3) move diagonally

Click a square to walk there. You stop if you see an enemy. Click an enemy to attack it.

F attack (or walk into the enemy) · Q dodge (attacks on you get disadvantage) · . wait

When an orc raises its axe, its target square turns red: step off it or dodge.

+ - or the mouse wheel zoom the map. X (or walking off the edge) leaves a town.

Double-click food or drink in your bag to eat it, the camping kit to sleep 8 hours. Tavern beds cost 1 gold (E on a bed).

Walk into a shopkeeper (pink @) to trade, or a villager to chat.

On the world map, B shows the three kingdoms' borders and their treasuries. Roads (·) are twice as quick. Walk into a caravan (C) to trade grain, stone, metal and luxury goods; the general store in every town trades them too, at prices that follow supply and demand.

E / Enter use: enter the dungeon, climb the stairs, open a chest

I open or close your bag

Drag items from a chest into your bag. Shift-click moves a whole stack.

Esc close windows / menu
